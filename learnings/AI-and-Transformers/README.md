# AI and Transformers

First learning track. Goal: a real, ground-up understanding of how modern AI
works — not a surface tour.

## Approach

Trace the field as a **historical timeline**. Understanding *why* each idea
appeared (what it fixed, what it couldn't do) is the fastest route to a durable
mental model — and to forming my own view of where models go next.

Two passes, interleaved:
1. **The story** — `timeline.md`: how we got from early ideas to transformers
   and today's models.
2. **The mechanics** — per-concept deep dives (attention, embeddings,
   backprop, etc.) as separate notes when a timeline entry needs unpacking.
