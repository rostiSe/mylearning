# AI and Transformers — Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Neural Networks and Deep Learning_ — Michael Nielsen](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/)
  Free online. Best technical introduction in existence. Builds from perceptrons through
  backprop to deep CNNs. Intuitive + rigorous. Use for: Lessons 1–4 (foundations through
  backprop). Read Chapter 1 alongside Lesson 0001.

- [Paper: "Attention Is All You Need" — Vaswani et al. (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
  The original transformer paper. Dense but readable with context. Use for: Lesson on
  the transformer architecture.

- [Book: _The Deep Learning Revolution_ — Terrence Sejnowski (2018)](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262038072/)
  Sejnowski was in the room for most of the connectionist revival. Excellent narrative
  history from someone who lived it. Use for: historical context on the AI winters,
  Boltzmann machines, and the backprop breakthrough.

- [Paper: "The Perceptron" — Frank Rosenblatt (1958)](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1959-09865-001)
  Psychological Review 65(6), 386–408. The original. Dense, but the core learning rule
  is readable. Use for: primary source for Lesson 0001.

- [Course: CS231n — Stanford (Fei-Fei Li et al.)](http://cs231n.stanford.edu/)
  Excellent notes and lectures on CNNs and deep learning. Free online notes are better
  than most books. Use for: Lessons on CNN / vision / AlexNet.

- [Course: CS224n — Stanford (Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning)](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)
  Covers word vectors, RNNs, attention, and transformers in sequence. Use for: Lessons
  6 onward (word2vec, RNN/LSTM, attention, transformer).

- [Paper: "Learning Representations by Back-propagating Errors" — Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams (1986)](https://www.nature.com/articles/323533a0)
  Nature. The paper that ended the first winter for multi-layer networks. Use for: Lesson
  on backpropagation.

- [Paper: "Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models" — Kaplan et al. (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361)
  The empirical foundation for the scaling era. Use for: Lesson on foundation models
  and scaling.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/MachineLearning](https://reddit.com/r/MachineLearning)
  Research-grade discussion. High signal; moderated against hype. Use for: paper
  discussion, sanity-checking interpretations.

- [Hacker News — #machinelearning](https://news.ycombinator.com)
  Good thread quality when research papers land. Practitioners comment. Use for:
  real-world reactions to new work.
